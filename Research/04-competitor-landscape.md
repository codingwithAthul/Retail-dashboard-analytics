# Competitor landscape around the UBC stores

## Method

1. List every retailer near each store that sells something SDM also sells (Verified from directories [1], [2]).
2. Map each competitor to SDM's categories: Pharmacy, OTC health, Beauty, Personal care, Grocery and fresh, Convenience food and drinks, Household.
3. For each category decide one action per store (Inferred):
   - **Compete**: SDM can win on range, services or PC Optimum.
   - **Differentiate**: compete but on something other than price.
   - **Avoid**: give the shelf space to a better category, because a nearby specialist wins on price.

## Category decisions (Inferred)

### SDM Wesbrook Village (#2247)

| Category | Main rival | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh produce, meat, bakery | Save-On-Foods | **Avoid** | A full grocery store is one block away with better price and range. Fresh would carry high shrink and lose on price. |
| Centre-store grocery | Save-On-Foods | **Avoid, keep top-ups only** | Keep milk, bread, eggs and snacks for convenience; do not fight on weekly-shop prices. |
| Pharmacy | Save-On pharmacy, PharmaChoice, Careville | **Differentiate** | Four pharmacies in one village. Win on services: vaccines, minor ailment prescribing, diabetes support, 8 AM to 10 PM hours, PC Optimum points. |
| Beauty (prestige) | None nearby | **Compete, expand** | No department store in walking distance. Prestige brands are already carried here. |
| OTC and personal care | Save-On-Foods | **Compete** | Range depth and pharmacist advice beat a grocery aisle. |
| Alcohol | BC Liquor | **Not applicable** | SDM stores in BC are not expected to sell alcohol (To verify). |
| Seasonal, gifts, cards | Limited | **Compete** | Residents need gifts and cards; few alternatives on campus. |

### SDM University of BC (#2273)

| Category | Main rival | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grab-and-go food and drinks | Campus food outlets | **Compete** | Highest-traffic location on campus; students buy between classes. |
| Pharmacy | University Pharmacy, UBC Pharmacists Clinic | **Differentiate** | Win on walk-in assessments (strep test, minor ailments) and long hours. |
| Value personal care (Life Brand, no name) | Limited | **Compete, expand** | Student budgets favour private label. |
| Prestige beauty | Limited | **Selective** | Mass beauty first; small prestige range for international students. |
| Fresh produce | Save-On-Foods (1.5 km) | **Avoid** | Students with time to cook shop at the grocery store. |
| Exam and back-to-school | None | **Compete, seasonal** | Stationery, energy drinks, cold and flu, cleaning supplies in September and exam months. |
