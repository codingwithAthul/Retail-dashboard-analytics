## Confidence labels

- **Verified**: stated directly by the cited source.
- **Inferred**: a reasoned conclusion from verified facts. Shown with the reasoning.
- **To verify**: plausible but not yet confirmed by a source. Must not be shown as fact in the dashboard.

## Data policy

- No internal Loblaw or Shoppers Drug Mart data was used. Sales figures in the dashboard are **synthetic sample data**, labelled as such in the UI.
- No employee names are published. Personnel fields are placeholders.
- Research gathered November 2026.
