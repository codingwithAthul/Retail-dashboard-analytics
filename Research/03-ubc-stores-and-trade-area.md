**Observations (Inferred)**

- The stores are about **1.5 km apart** in a straight line (computed from the map markers).
- Both store pages list the **same Associate-owner**. The two stores are one owner's portfolio, so the dashboard should help that owner and head office *split roles* between them instead of having them compete.
- The beauty brand lists already differ: **prestige brands at Wesbrook, mass brands on University Blvd**. That matches a resident / higher-income customer at Wesbrook and a student customer near the academic core.
- The University Blvd store offers two extra clinical services (strep test, A1C test), which suits walk-in students and staff.

**Wesbrook Village** is UBC's residential neighbourhood at the south of campus. It holds the Save-On-Foods grocery anchor, a BC Liquor store, dental, chiropractic and physio clinics, banks, cafés and services, with free short-term street parking and an underground parkade [6].

**University Boulevard / University Village** is the commercial strip at the campus core, close to the academic buildings and student residences, with restaurants, a gym, dental and optical clinics and a telecom store [6].
