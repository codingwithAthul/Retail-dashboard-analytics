# Shoppers Drug Mart: business model and store formats

## Summary

Shoppers Drug Mart (SDM) is Loblaw's pharmacy banner: more than 1,300 stores across Canada selling "health, wellness, beauty and household essentials" [3]. Most stores are owned and run by a pharmacist **Associate-owner** inside the SDM system [2].

The words "small / standalone / large" are close but not the terms SDM uses. The format names that sources use are:

| Format (term used in this project) | What it is | Typical size | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conventional** | The classic SDM drug store, often in a mall, strip plaza or street-front unit | Network average about 6,900 sq ft in 2002 | [4] |
| **Large format** | Introduced 2002. More space, stronger cosmetics focus, cosmetics at the entrance, pharmacy at the back, "Food Essentials" near the front cash. In suburbs usually a **stand-alone big-box** building | Pushed the network average to about 9,400 sq ft by 2008 | [1], [4] |
| **Urban / mixed-use** | Street-level unit in a residential or campus building. Not a named SDM format; used here to describe the UBC stores (Inferred) | Varies | Inferred |
| **Shoppers Simply Pharmacy** | Pharmacy-only unit inside medical buildings and clinics, limited OTC and vitamins | About 1,000 sq ft; 49 locations | [2], [5] |
| **Shoppers Home Health Care** | Mobility and home medical equipment | 56 locations | [1] |
| **Pharmaprix** | The Quebec name for SDM since 1972 | Same formats | [1], [3] |

Departments and concepts that sit **inside** stores:

- **Beauty Boutique**: prestige cosmetics department, in 440 stores [2].
- **Food / convenience**: convenience food departments in 600+ stores by 2009 [4].
- **Post office counters**: in nearly 600 stores (2009) [4].
- **Clinics**: Beauty Clinic by Shoppers (2018), Health Clinic by Shoppers (2020) [3].
- **Murale** (2009, prestige beauty) and **Wellwise** (2017, seniors' healthy living; sold to Verillium Health Care in 2025) were stand-alone concepts [1], [4].

## Business model (Verified)

- Founded 1962 by pharmacist Murray Koffler. Pharmacist "associates would own and operate their own stores within the system and share in the profits" [1].
- "More than 1,288 Associate-owned locations" [2].
- Acquired by Loblaw in 2014 for CA$12.4 billion [1], [3].
- In 2012 SDM said 51% of purchases were non-pharmaceutical (front store) [1].
- 2009 market share: 14.9% of front-store market, about 20.3% of prescriptions [4].

## Mission and target customer

- Tagline "Your Life Store" since 2002; later "Live Life Well" [1], [3].
- Core offer is "health, beauty and convenience" [2], [4].
- SDM planned growth around ageing Canadians: people aged 60 to 79 rising from 5.1 million (2008) to 8.7 million (2026) [4].
- Also planned to "dense up" in Canada's top 10 urban markets; 400+ stores open to midnight or 24 hours (2009) [4].

## Why format matters for the dashboard (Inferred)

- **Format sets the assortment ceiling.** A large-format store can carry a full Beauty Boutique, a food aisle and seasonal goods. A small urban unit cannot, so it must choose the categories that pay best per square foot.
- **Front store vs pharmacy mix** is the main financial difference between stores. A campus store with young customers is likely to lean on front store (snacks, personal care, cosmetics) while a store near families and seniors leans on prescriptions. This is a hypothesis to test with real sales data.
- The dashboard therefore shows **Type of store** in the Distinguishables section and uses category mix (not total sales) as the headline sales view.
